# Prompt Template – Image to Video

## Core principle
Ưu tiên Image-to-Video với ảnh tham chiếu để đảm bảo tính nhất quán nhân vật.

## Workflow
1. Dùng ảnh reference DAVI dạng front, side, 3/4.
2. Tạo scene chính với cùng phong cách thiết kế.
3. Chỉ thay đổi biểu cảm, cử chỉ, bối cảnh, góc quay.
4. Mỗi cảnh dài 5–10 giây.

## Template

DAVI, a cute premium 3D animated green alien mascot,
with a large smooth round lime-green head,
oversized glossy deep teal-black eyes,
large expressive pupils with white reflections,
two subtle curved eyebrows,
a tiny nose,
a small friendly smiling mouth,
two small green hands,
a short delicate antenna on top of the head
with a small round green glowing tip,
a compact adorable childlike body proportion,
wearing a futuristic silver-white astronaut spacesuit,
with subtle metallic blue and cyan details,
a small illuminated technology control panel
on the center of the chest,
blue metallic wrist and ankle accents,
futuristic silver astronaut boots.

DAVI is placed in a clean and futuristic financial studio environment.
The character performs a smooth natural gesture while maintaining the same recognizable design.
Cinematic camera movement, soft realistic lighting, premium 3D animation style,
vertical 9:16 composition.
